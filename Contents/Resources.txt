# Resources for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing People in Bristol

We are here to help you connect with the local services, organizations, and community support available to you in Bristol. Whether you are looking for equipment, professional support, or just a friendly face, there is a vibrant network ready to assist you.

---

## Local Support Organizations

### Centre for Deaf and Hard of Hearing People (CfD)
A community-based charity providing information, equipment, community groups, and training for Deaf and hard-of-hearing (HoH) people in Bristol.
*   **Website:** [cfd.org.uk](https://cfd.org.uk)
*   **Phone (Office):** 0117 939 8653
*   **Phone (Equipment):** 0345 900 7830
*   **SMS/WhatsApp:** 07545 264 840
*   **Email (Office):** office@cfd.org.uk
*   **Email (Equipment):** equipment@cfd.org.uk
*   **Address:** Vassall Centre, Gill Avenue, Bristol BS16 2QQ
*   **Services:** They offer a resource room, equipment assessments, and various local community groups and activities tailored to the Deaf and HoH community.

### Bristol City Council – Sensory Support Service (Adults)
The Adult Social Care team provides advice, rehabilitation, and Care Act assessments for Deaf, hard-of-hearing, Deafblind, and visually impaired adults in Bristol.
*   **Email:** sensory.impairment@bristol.gov.uk
*   **SMS:** 07775 646 671
*   **Drop-in Sessions:**
    *   **Wednesdays:** 1:30–3:30 pm at CfD (Vassall Centre, BS16 2QQ)
    *   **Fridays:** 1:30–3:30 pm at Bedminster Library, BS3 4AQ
*   **Services:** They can help with booking appointments, managing daily tasks, and referrals to other specialist services. Video (Zoom) support is available for BSL users.

## Resources for Children and Families

### Sensory Support Service (Children and Families)
Education-focused support for children and young people with hearing loss and their families.
*   **Website:** [sensorysupportservice.org.uk](http://sensorysupportservice.org.uk/)
*   **Phone:** 0117 903 8441 / 8442 / 8443
*   **Address:** Elmfield House, Greystoke Avenue, Westbury-on-Trym, Bristol BS10 6AY
*   **Specialist Group:** *Acorns Early Years Group* is a specialist pre-school group for children aged 0–5 and their families.

### Family Centre for Deaf Children (FCDC)
A charity that provides activities, advice, sign-language classes, and events specifically for families of deaf children.
*   **Website:** [fcdc.org.uk](http://www.fcdc.org.uk)
*   **Phone:** 0117 330 7575
*   **Text:** 07919 575247
*   **Email:** admin@fcdc.org.uk

### Bristol Deaf Children’s Society (NDCS Local Group)
A parent-led group affiliated with the National Deaf Children’s Society, offering peer support and advice to families across Bristol.
*   **Email:** bristol@ndcsgroup.org.uk
*   **Community:** You can find details about their Facebook group via the NDCS local group listing.
