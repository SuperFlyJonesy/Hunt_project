# Partner with the Bristol Hearing Loss Initiative

We are a grassroots, community-led project dedicated to building practical support, training, and social spaces for Bristol’s hearing loss initiates. We know that hearing loss can often feel isolating, but we are working to change that by fostering connection and accessibility across our city.

By partnering with us, you are not just funding a project; you are directly removing barriers for thousands of local people. Together, we can make Bristol a more inclusive place to live, work, and socialize, while helping your organization reach and support this important community.

## Why Partner with Us?

*   **Meaningful Local Impact:** With over 62,000 adults in Bristol experiencing some level of hearing loss, your support has a direct, tangible impact on the daily lives of local people.
*   **Authentic, Community-Led Leadership:** Our initiative is led by people with lived experience of hearing loss. We design solutions *with* our community, not just for them, ensuring our work is genuine and truly meets people's needs.
*   **Shared Mission:** Whether you are a hearing technology provider looking to extend your mission beyond your products, or a local business wanting to champion accessibility, our partnership aligns your work with a clear, positive social impact.

## What Your Support Makes Possible

Your partnership directly funds the essential work that keeps our community connected:

*   **Accessible Community Spaces:** We hire venues across Bristol that are truly accessible, ensuring our meetups and events are welcoming and low-stress.
*   **Empowering Training:** We bring in expert guest speakers to deliver training on hearing loss awareness for local workplaces and community venues.
*   **Resources for All:** We develop and share digital guides and resources that help families, employers, and organizations become more inclusive.

## How You Can Partner with Us

We believe in building partnerships that work for everyone. We would love to tailor a support package that fits your organization’s goals. Typical partnership options include:

*   **Community Partnership:** Fund our regular community meetups and be recognized as a key supporter on our website and at our events.
*   **Training Partnership:** Underwrite our hearing loss awareness training sessions for local workplaces and venues, and collaborate on shared resources.
*   **Accessibility Partnership:** Support specific accessibility improvements, such as hearing loops, captioning, or transport bursaries to ensure everyone can attend our events.
