# Join Our Community: Building a More Accessible Bristol Together

Welcome! We are building a movement to make Bristol a city where everyone feels included, understood, and able to enjoy everything our vibrant community has to offer, regardless of hearing loss.

We believe that being "hard of hearing" (HoH) should never be a barrier to participating fully in life. However, navigating a city when you aren't sure if a venue will be accessible can be exhausting. We are here to change that, and we would love for you to join us.

## How You Can Help

By joining our Patreon community, you are directly funding the vital, on-the-ground work that helps us map accessibility across Bristol. Your support allows us to:

*   **Visit and Assess Venues:** We visit local bars, cafés, restaurants, and community spaces to see how welcoming they truly are.
*   **Check Accessibility:** We assess noise levels, lighting, and whether they have working, staff-supported hearing loops or other assistive systems.
*   **Share Real-Time Insights:** We share updates, vlogs, and posts so our community knows exactly which venues are ready to welcome them.

Your contributions cover essential costs—like travel, equipment, and editing—that allow us to keep building an honest, community-led picture of Bristol's accessibility.

## The "HoH Gold Standard"

We want to make it easy for venues to know how they can welcome our community. We have developed the **HoH Gold Standard checklist**, based on national guidance. A venue that achieves Gold Standard status is one that is truly deaf-aware.

### What Makes a "Gold Standard" Venue?

*   **Reliable Hearing Loops:** They have a functional, well-maintained hearing loop or assistive system (like Auracast) in key areas, and it is clearly signposted.
*   **Confident, Trained Staff:** Their staff don't just know the equipment exists—they know how to switch it on and feel confident communicating with HoH visitors.
*   **HoH-Friendly Environment:** They prioritize good, clear lighting (so lip-reading is easier), manage background music sensibly, and ensure key information (like menus or event times) is available in written form.
*   **Clear Information:** They proudly advertise their accessibility features on their website or social media, making it easy for you to plan your visit with confidence.

## Building the Bristol HoH Map

With the support of our Patreon community, we are building a live map of Bristol—documenting the good, the bad, and the places that need a little help to become truly accessible.

We log our results online, so you can quickly and easily see:
*   Which loops and assistive systems actually work.
*   Where staff are deaf-aware and use helpful communication techniques.
*   Which venues offer an environment (noise, lighting, layout) that makes your visit easy and enjoyable.

Over time, this map becomes more than just a resource—it’s a powerful community tool that offers a gentle, encouraging nudge for venues to improve their accessibility.
