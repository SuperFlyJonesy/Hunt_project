hearing Hearing Aids Guide & Styles

Choosing and learning to use a hearing aid is a positive step toward staying connected. Discover device styles, maintenance essentials, and expert talks.

Modern hearing aids come in a variety of styles tailored to your specific hearing prescription, ear shape, and personal preferences:

BTE (Behind-the-Ear)

A versatile and popular style that sits discreetly behind the ear, delivering sound through a slim tube or custom mould.

RIC (Receiver-in-Canal)

Extremely popular; places the speaker directly in the canal for natural acoustics and zero occlusion.

ITE & ITC (In-the-Ear / Canal)

Custom-moulded to fit snug within your outer ear or canal for discreet daily use.

CROS & BiCROS

Transfers sound wirelessly from an unaidable ear over to your better-hearing ear, eliminating blind spots.

Taking a few moments to care for your hearing aid ensures crisp sound quality and prevents feedback whistling:

Earmould Retubing (Every 4–6 Months)

Thin Tube & Dome Cleaning

Daily Care Checklist:

  • Daily Wipe: Wipe moulds and domes with a dry microfiber cloth; never use boiling water or harsh chemicals.
  • Battery Hygiene: Open battery doors overnight to let moisture evaporate and extend battery life.
  • T-Loop Programme: Use your telecoil ('T') setting in banks, theatres, and churches equipped with hearing loop systems.

Need Replacement Batteries, Tubing, or Domes?

If you are a patient of the Adult Audiology service at University Hospitals Bristol and Weston (UHBW), you can now order replacement batteries (P13 Orange, 312 Brown, 675 Blue), mould tubing, thin tubes/domes, and cleaning wires online for free home delivery.

📮 Delivery: Sent via Royal Mail 2nd Class within 2 weeks. (Please only request batteries when you are about to run out to help preserve NHS resources).
