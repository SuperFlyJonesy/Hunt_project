Hearing Aids

Choosing and learning to use a hearing aid is a positive step toward staying connected to the people and sounds you love. Here is a brief guide to the different types available.

Types of Hearing Aids

  • BTE (Behind-the-Ear): A versatile, popular style that sits discreetly behind the ear.
  • ITE (In-the-Ear): Custom-made to fit comfortably within the outer ear.
  • ITC (In-the-Canal): A compact design that sits securely inside the ear canal.
  • Body Worn: A powerful solution designed for those with significant hearing challenges. It helps ensure speech is clear and comfortable to hear.

Caring for Your Device

Taking a few moments to care for your hearing aid helps ensure it continues to provide you with the best possible sound quality.

  • Daily Cleaning: A quick wipe keeps your device clean and working well.
  • Regular Checks: Simply changing batteries and checking tubes keeps things running smoothly.
  • Getting Help: If you're unsure about cleaning or retubing, your audiologist is always happy to guide you through it.
  • Using Induction Loops: Many hearing aids have a special programme setting—often called a 'T-setting'—that helps you hear more clearly in public spaces with an induction loop system.
