help_center Step-by-Step: How to Sign Up on Patreon

volunteer_activism Go to Join Us Page →

Becoming a Patreon member (A Pardon, Initiate, or A Hunter) unlocks exclusive perks like our official Hearing Loss Gold Standard Checklist, live Bristol HoH Map access, and assistive tech reviews. Follow these 4 simple steps:

1

Select Your Tier

Go to our Join Us page and click your preferred tier button (A Pardon £3/mo, Initiate £6/mo, or A Hunter £12/mo).

2

Create Free Account

On Patreon's official website, sign in or create a quick free account using your Email, Google, or Apple ID.

3

Confirm Secure Checkout

Enter your payment details (Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, or Apple Pay) via Patreon's 256-bit encrypted checkout.

4

Unlock Perks Instantly

You're all set! You immediately gain access to the Gold Standard Checklist PDF, member map updates, and voting rights.
