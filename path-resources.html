Hearing Impaired Support Scheme (HISS Bristol)

A Bristol registered charity working in partnership with UHBW NHS Audiology. Trained volunteers (many who use hearing aids themselves) provide home visits for elderly or housebound NHS hearing aid users to assist with retubing, cleaning, battery supply, and advice.

🏠 Home Visit Maintenance & Referral:

Provides free home visits for UHBW NHS hearing aid users unable to attend clinic due to mobility, age, or disability.

  • call Referral: Contact UHBW Audiology for HISS referral
  • home Services: Free Home Tube Replacement, Cleaning & Battery Support
