Ways You Can Support Our Programme

☕

Group Session Host & Greeter

Help welcome attendees to our Bristol meetups, serve free tea and coffee, and ensure every newcomer receives a friendly, supportive reception.

🎙️

Hearing Loop & Tech Facilitator

Assist with setting up portable hearing loops, testing wireless microphones, and optimizing venue acoustics for smooth communication.

🤝

Peer Supporter & Listener

Share lived experience, offer encouragement to those navigating hearing loss grief, and help connect initiates with local services.

📍

Outreach & Event Ambassador

Represent the initiative at Bristol community fairs, libraries, and venue pop-ups to raise awareness and distribute resources.
