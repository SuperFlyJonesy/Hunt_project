Grassroots Corporate Partnership

corporate_fare Partner with the Bristol Hearing Loss Initiative

We’re a grassroots, community-led project run by people with lived hard-of-hearing (HoH) experience, building practical support, workplace training, and accessible social spaces across Bristol.

By sponsoring us, your organization directly removes real barriers for over 62,000 local adults with hearing loss while demonstrating authentic commitment to inclusion and accessibility.

Direct Community Impact

volunteer_activism What Your Funding Makes Possible

  • Accessible Venue Hires: Funding meetups in venues equipped with verified hearing loop systems and low-reverberation acoustics.
  • Welcoming Environments: Providing free hot refreshments, clear lighting, and live captions for all attendees.
  • Workplace & Venue Training: Delivering accredited hearing loss awareness training for local Bristol businesses and public venues.
  • Digital Guides & Resources: Publishing verified support materials for employees, employers, and families across the West of England.
