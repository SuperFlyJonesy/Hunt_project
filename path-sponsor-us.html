We are a grassroots community project run by people with lived experience of hearing loss, building practical support, workplace training, and accessible social spaces across Bristol.

By sponsoring us, your organisation directly removes real barriers for over 62,000 local adults with hearing loss while demonstrating authentic commitment to inclusion and accessibility.

  • Community Support Group: Funding the venue hire and guest speakers to keep our evening support group completely free for all Bristol residents.
  • Welcoming Environments: Providing hot refreshments, clear lighting for lipreading, and assistive listening systems for all attendees.
  • Workplace & Venue Training: Delivering dedicated staff training sessions on clear communication and practical accessibility for local employers and venues.
  • Digital Guides & Public Mapping: Maintaining our free public venue accessibility directory and roadmaps for employees, employers, and families.
