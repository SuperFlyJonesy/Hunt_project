Why We Need Your Support

Navigating a city when you aren't sure if a venue has working hearing loops or deaf-aware staff can be exhausting. Your Patreon contributions directly fund on-the-ground venue assessments across Bristol, equipment testing, and community-led accessibility logging.

stars What Your Pledge Directly Funds:

  • On-Site Venue Audits: Visiting local bars, cafés, restaurants, and venues to test noise levels, lighting, and hearing loops.
  • Staff Training & Awareness: Educating venue staff on deaf-aware communication (like PACE: Patience, Attention, Clarity, Ear contact).
  • The Bristol HoH Accessibility Map: Maintaining a free, live online map so anyone can check where assistive loops work before leaving home.
  • Assistive Tech Reviews: Testing new speech-to-text apps, TV sound streamers, and Phonak Roger systems with community input.

The Hearing Loss Gold Standard

Our **Hearing Loss Gold Standard** gives venues a clear, practical checklist to become genuinely accessible. A Gold Standard venue guarantees:

  • Reliable Hearing Loops: Correctly installed hearing loop or Auracast system at counters and main seating areas.
  • Deaf-Aware Staff: Staff trained in basic communication (Patience, Attention, Clarity, Ear contact).
  • HoH Environment: Good facial lighting, managed background noise, and written menus or event details.
