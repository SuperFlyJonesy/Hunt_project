Navigating a city when you aren't sure if a venue has working hearing loops or staff trained in hearing loss communication can be exhausting. Your Patreon contributions directly fund on-the-ground venue assessments across Bristol:

What Your Pledge Directly Funds:
  • On-Site Venue Audits: Visiting local bars, cafés, restaurants, and venues to test noise levels, lighting, and hearing loops.
  • Staff Training & Awareness: Educating venue staff on clear communication and awareness (using the PACE framework: Patience, Attention, Clarity, Ear Contact).
  • The Accessible Venues Directory: Maintaining our comprehensive directory and map so anyone can check venue loop facilities and acoustic ratings before leaving home.
  • Assistive Tech Reviews: Testing new speech-to-text apps, TV sound streamers, and Phonak Roger systems with community input.

Our Hearing Loss Gold Standard gives venues a clear, practical checklist to become genuinely accessible:

  • Reliable Hearing Loops: Correctly installed hearing loop or Auracast system at counters and main seating areas.
  • Trained Staff: Staff trained in clear hearing loss communication (PACE framework).
  • Acoustic & Visual Environment: Good facial lighting, managed background noise, and written menus or event details.
