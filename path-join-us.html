Our absolute biggest priority is running a 100% free evening support group in Bristol. community pledges directly fund the room hire, guest speakers, and tea and coffee to keep this group free for everyone.

What Your Pledge Directly Funds:
  • Free Evening Support Group: Funding the room hire so anyone in Bristol living with hearing loss can attend at zero cost.
  • Guest Speakers & Sessions: Bringing in expert lipreading teachers, hearing therapists, and technology specialists to share practical advice.
  • Tea, Coffee & Refreshments: Keeping the kettle boiling and refreshments free so members can chat, share experiences, and connect without pressure.
  • Frontline Venue Audits: Visiting local cafés, pubs, and community spaces to test noise levels, lighting, and hearing loops for our free public map.

Our Hearing Loss Gold Standard gives Bristol venues a clear, practical roadmap to become genuinely accessible for everyone. Community members can suggest local spots directly in our Discord to prioritise them for our audit pipeline.

What Our Gold Standard Audit Pipeline Checks:
  • Reliable Hearing Loops: Working hearing loop or Auracast broadcast systems at counters, reception desks, and main seating areas.
  • Trained Staff: Staff trained in clear hearing loss communication using our simple PACE guidelines (Patience, Attention, Clarity, Eye Contact).
  • Acoustic & Visual Environment: Clear lighting for lipreading, managed background noise, and printed or digital menus.
