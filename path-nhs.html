local_hospital Combined Organization Overview

The Bristol NHS trust delivers combined NHS Audiology & ENT care across University Hospitals Bristol and Weston NHS Foundation Trust (UHBW) and North Bristol NHS Trust (NBT). Together, they provide seamless hearing assessments, hearing aid fittings, tinnitus therapy, and balance services across Bristol and the West of England.

info First-Time Visitor Essentials

🚗 Bristol Clean Air Zone (CAZ) Notice:
St Michael's Hospital and the central hospital precinct sit inside the Bristol Clean Air Zone. Non-compliant vehicles are subject to a daily charge when driving to St Michael's Hill. Check your vehicle compliance on the official Bristol City Council CAZ tool before travelling. (Note: Southmead Hospital and Weston General Hospital are outside the central CAZ zone).
🦻 Hearing Loop & Accessibility Support:
All main Audiology waiting rooms at St Michael's Hospital, Southmead Brunel Building (Gate 36), and Weston General Hospital feature induction hearing loops, clear digital signage, and accessible seating. If you require a BSL interpreter or speech-to-text reporter for your appointment, contact the department in advance.

🧭 Step-by-Step Internal Directions to Audiology:

How to find Adult Audiology inside St Michael's Hospital:
  1. From St Michael's Hill Main Entrance (Level E): Enter main reception on Level E. Head towards the central lift bank/stairwell and descend 2 floors down to Level C.
  2. From Southwell Street Courtyard Entrance (Level C): Enter via the dedicated Level C courtyard entrance (set back from St Michael's Hill near the NIHR Research Facility). This entrance provides step-free, flat access straight into Level C.
  3. Follow Zone Signs: Follow the blue line floor indicator and overhead "Audiology Outpatients / Hearing Aids" signs along the Level C main clinical corridor.
  4. Reception & Waiting Room: Audiology reception desk and waiting room are on your left along the main Level C corridor.

🧭 Step-by-Step Internal Directions to Gate 36 Audiology:

How to find Gate 36 Audiology inside the Brunel Building:
  1. Enter Main Atrium: Enter through the main entrance of the Brunel Building into the Level 1 central indoor street (Atrium).
  2. Walk Along Central Street: Walk straight ahead along the central glass-roof atrium. Gates are numbered sequentially on overhead banners.
  3. Locate Gate 36 Landmark: Continue past Costa Coffee and M&S. Look for Gate 36 on your right, situated directly opposite the League of Friends Coffee Shop.
  4. Check-in at Reception: Walk through Gate 36 doors to the Audiology reception desk and patient waiting area.

🧭 Step-by-Step Internal Directions to Audiology:

How to find Audiology inside Weston General Hospital:
  1. Enter Main Entrance: Enter through the main hospital building entrance off Grange Road onto the ground floor corridor.
  2. Pass Costa Coffee: Walk past the Costa Coffee seating area on your right towards the main Outpatients reception.
  3. Follow Outpatients Signs: Continue straight down the main Outpatients corridor. Overhead directional signs point clearly to "Audiology & Hearing Aids" at the end of the clinical wing.
  4. Check-in at Reception: Audiology reception desk and waiting room are situated on the ground floor at the end of the Outpatients corridor.

assignment_ind Referrals to NHS Audiology

GP Referrals: Your GP will inspect your ear canals, assess symptoms, and submit a direct NHS referral to UHBW (St Michael's / Weston General) or North Bristol NHS Trust (Southmead).

ENT & Specialist Referrals: Ear, Nose & Throat (ENT) consultants can refer directly for specialized adult audiology, tinnitus management, or balance therapy.
