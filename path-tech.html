📶 Auracast & LE Audio Bluetooth SIG & Network Rail 🕒 Updated Today

podcasts Auracast™ Public Station Trial at Bristol Temple Meads

Bristol Temple Meads is hosting the world's first public rail station trial of Auracast™ broadcast audio. This new Bluetooth® LE Audio standard broadcasts crystal-clear train announcements and safety alerts directly to passenger hearing devices simultaneously.

Developed in partnership with Centre for Deaf and Hard of Hearing People (CfD Bristol), Connected Places Catapult, and Ampetronic, eliminating background station reverberation.

🧬 Gene Therapy Nature Medicine & Otonomy Studies 🕒 Verified Research

biotech Inner Ear Hair Cell Gene Therapy Clinical Breakthrough

Clinical trials published in Nature Medicine demonstrate successful AAV-mediated gene therapy restoring functioning otoferlin (OTOF) protein inside cochlear hair cells, restoring auditory thresholds for congenital and sensorineural loss.

Researchers are extending trials to target age-related (presbyacusis) stereocilia hair cell regeneration, unlocking biological reversal of sensory hearing loss.

memory AI Neural Noise Isolation & On-Chip Voice Separation

📶 Public Accessibility UK National Venues & Theatre Association 🕒 UK Adoption

theater_comedy UK Cultural Venues & Theatre Auracast Rollout

Major venues across the UK — including The National Theatre, Shakespeare’s Globe, and Bristol Beacon — are adopting multi-channel Auracast broadcast loops.

Attendees can stream live audio, audio descriptions, or localized translations straight to their personal hearing aids or smartphones without renting external neckloops.
