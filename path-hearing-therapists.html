Comprehensive directory of specialist NHS Hearing Therapists, audiology rehabilitation teams, and community communication advisers across Bristol.

Hearing Therapists specialise in helping adults manage the personal, emotional, and social impact of hearing loss, tinnitus, and auditory processing conditions. Unlike hearing aid dispensers who focus solely on technology, hearing therapists provide holistic rehabilitation and practical coping strategies.

🗣️ Communication Tactics Lipreading skills, managing background noise, and requesting accommodations.
🔔 Tinnitus & Hyperacusis Sound therapy, relaxation techniques, habituation, and distress reduction.
❤️ Emotional Support Navigating hearing loss grief, social anxiety, communication fatigue, and isolation.
📱 Assistive Technology Integrating hearing aids with Roger mics, TV streamers, and flashing alerts.
