A quiet, structured space built for real conversations

Discord works on your smartphone, tablet, or desktop web browser. Unlike standard social networks, Discord has zero advertising algorithms, public metrics, or distracting feeds. Conversations stay organised into separate topic rooms called channels.

We set up dedicated channels so Bristol residents navigating hearing loss can read and write at their own pace without competing against background noise or social fatigue.

Hearing Loss Initiative is a grassroots peer project built for Bristol. Our mission is to break down isolation, share practical survival strategies, and ensure public venues take acoustic accessibility seriously.

How You Can Support the Initiative:
  • Attend Free Evening Meetups: Join our upcoming in-person peer sessions in central Bristol to share lived experiences and coping strategies.
  • Audit Local Spaces: Test hearing loop clarity, sound reverberation, and lighting in your local Bristol cafés, pubs, and venues.
  • Contribute Knowledge: Share your NHS audiology experiences, assistive gadget tips, and lipreading techniques in our online forum.
  • Advocate for Gold Standards: Help us encourage local Bristol venues to install certified hearing loops and train front-of-house staff.

Our Hearing Loss Gold Standard gives Bristol venues a clear, practical benchmark to become accessible. Initiates visit local spaces, test facilities, and share findings.

What Our Initiative Audits Check:
  • Reliable Hearing Loops: Working hearing loop or Auracast broadcast systems at counters, reception desks, and main seating areas.
  • Trained Staff: Frontline teams trained in clear communication using our PACE guidelines (Patience, Attention, Clarity, Eye Contact).
  • Acoustic & Visual Environment: Clear lighting for lipreading, managed background noise, and printed or digital menus.
