# Hearing Loss Awareness Training for Bristol Workplaces

Give your team the confidence to communicate effectively with people who are deaf or hard of hearing—whether they are colleagues, customers, patients, students, or visitors.

Our training is developed and delivered by Jason and our specialist member initiates, drawing on years of professional experience at Bristol’s Centre for Deaf and Hard of Hearing People and lived experience of hearing loss.

---

## Why Inclusive Communication Matters

In Bristol, over 62,000 adults are estimated to have some form of hearing loss—that’s roughly 16% of our adult population. This means hearing loss is already a part of your workplace, even if no one has mentioned it.

When hearing loss isn't recognized or supported, individuals can feel excluded, miss key information, and experience significant fatigue from the effort of trying to listen. By making a few simple, thoughtful changes, you can create a more inclusive, productive, and welcoming environment for everyone.

## What Your Team Will Learn

Our sessions are practical, conversational, and focused on real-world situations. We start from the basics, so no prior knowledge is needed. By the end of the session, your team will feel confident to:

*   **Recognize the Signs:** Understand when someone might be struggling to hear, and how to offer support respectfully.
*   **Communicate Effectively:** Learn to get someone’s attention, face them properly, and speak clearly without shouting or "over-simplifying."
*   **Support in Noisy Spaces:** Practical tips for making busy offices, cafés, receptions, and event venues more accessible.
*   **Run Inclusive Meetings:** Implement simple steps for more deaf-aware meetings and video calls.
*   **Signpost Local Support:** Know where to direct people for further help (such as CfD, RNID, the NHS, and local sensory support services).

We always include time for questions and encourage your staff to share examples from their own roles to make the learning feel relevant and practical.
