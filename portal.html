Bristol Business & Workplace Accessibility

Practical guidance for local employers, venues, and customer service teams

Over 62,220 Bristol adults live with hearing loss. Implementing straightforward adjustments transforms customer experience, employee retention, and Equality Act compliance.

hearing Hearing Loop Standard

Ensuring compliance with BS EN 60118-4 acoustic standards for counter loops and meeting rooms.

  • Position microphones within 20cm of staff mouth.
  • Conduct monthly signal testing with a field strength receiver.
  • Display clear, high-contrast loop signage at all reception points.

currency_pound Access to Work Scheme

UK Government funding (up to £66,000/yr) covering assistive technology for employees at zero net cost to employers.

  • 100% funding for Roger On microphones & table transmitters.
  • Live speech-to-text transcription software licenses.
  • Professional workplace acoustic ergonomic assessments.

record_voice_over Staff Deaf Awareness

Practical communication etiquette for customer service desks and internal team meetings.

  • Face the person directly in good lighting; do not cover your mouth.
  • Speak clearly at normal volume; avoid shouting or exaggerated mouthings.
  • Enable automated live captions on MS Teams and Zoom calls by default.

volume_down Acoustic Environment

Low-cost workplace modifications that dramatically improve speech intelligibility.

  • Introduce soft wall panels, curtains, or acoustic desk dividers.
  • Designate quiet meeting pods away from high-traffic coffee machines.
  • Manage background music levels during peak service hours.
