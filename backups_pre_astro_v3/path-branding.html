Brand Voice & Accessibility Principles

1. WCAG 2.2 AA Contrast

Every text color must maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast against its background. All focus rings must be visible in 3px solid #005EB8 or #00A4EF.

2. Lived Experience Voice

Warm, empowering, plain English, respectful. Focus on practical real-world solutions rather than clinical jargon or pity.

3. PACE Framework Standard

Always uphold the 4 pillars: Patience, Attention, Clarity, Eye Contact across all training materials and signage.
