📶 Auracast & LE Audio Bluetooth SIG & Network Rail 🕒 Updated Today

Bristol Temple Meads is trialling Auracast™ broadcast audio. This Bluetooth® LE Audio standard sends train announcements directly to hearing aids and earbuds without background station echo.

Built with the Centre for Deaf and Hard of Hearing People (CfD Bristol), Connected Places Catapult, and Ampetronic.

🧬 Gene Therapy Nature Medicine 🕒 Verified Study

Gene therapy trials published in Nature Medicine show progress in repairing inner ear hair cells. Replacing faulty OTOF genes in clinical trials helped restore natural hearing responses.

Researchers are expanding trials to test hair cell regeneration for age-related hearing loss.

🧠 AI Noise Filter IEEE Spectrum 🕒 2026 Update

Modern hearing aids use AI chips to separate speech from background noise. The processor isolates the person speaking in front of you while lowering background chatter.

This helps improve conversation clarity in busy restaurants and public spaces without muffling voices.

📶 Public Venues UK Theatres & Venues 🕒 UK Adoption

UK venues including The National Theatre, Shakespeare's Globe, and Bristol Beacon are installing Auracast broadcast systems.

Visitors can stream show audio or audio descriptions straight to their hearing aids or phones without picking up a neckloop.
