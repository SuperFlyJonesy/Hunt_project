Who We Are & Mission

Hi, we are Jason and Jo. As former CfD employees and hearing loss initiates, we understand firsthand the barriers, frustration, and isolation that can accompany hearing loss.

We built this initiative to create an empowering, practical support network across Bristol—helping people reconnect with work, family, social life, and public spaces with complete confidence.

🌱

Grassroots & Community Funded

This is a community-driven project where every contribution directly enables real-world accessibility:

  • Hire accessible venues with verified hearing loops and acoustic setups.
  • Provide free refreshments so everyone gets a warm welcome and a free coffee.
  • Host expert speakers and deliver specialist accessibility training for local venues.

This community-funded model bridges the gap while securing long-term council and corporate backing.
