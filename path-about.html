groups About Our Initiative

Built by lived experience to create a safe, accessible, and practical support network for hearing loss initiates across Bristol.

Hi, we are Jason and Jo. As former Centre for Deaf and Hard of Hearing People (CfD) staff and lifelong hearing loss initiates, we understand firsthand the barriers, communication fatigue, frustration, and isolation that can accompany hearing loss.

We founded the Hearing Loss Initiative to create an empowering, practical, and welcoming support network across Bristol, helping people reconnect with work, family, social life, and public spaces with complete confidence.

👂 Lived Experience First

Every guide, checklist, and resource is created from direct personal experience with hearing aids, lipreading, and auditory fatigue.

🏛️ Bristol-Wide Access

Auditing local venues, verifying hearing loop performance, and advocating for inclusive acoustic environments across Bristol.

🤝 Community & Connection

Providing peer support, meetups, and a safe space where nobody has to struggle or nod along in silence.

💡 Practical Education

Equipping employers, healthcare professionals, and families with practical communication frameworks like PACE.
