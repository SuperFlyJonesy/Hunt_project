Our Mission & Lived Experience

Hi, I am Jason. As a former Centre for Deaf and Hard of Hearing People (CfD) specialist and lifelong hearing loss initiate, I understand firsthand the barriers, communication fatigue, frustration, and isolation that can accompany hearing loss in daily life.

I founded the Hearing Loss Initiative to build the bridge that was missing in Bristol: connecting NHS clinical care with practical real-world support, verified assistive listening technology (including Bluetooth Auracast), acoustic venue auditing, and welcoming peer connection.

👂 Lived Experience First

Every guide, checklist, and roadmap is shaped by authentic lived experience with digital hearing aids, lipreading, and listening strain.

🏛️ Bristol-Wide Access

Auditing local venues & assessments (coming soon), verifying hearing loops, and championing quiet acoustics across the city.

🤝 Community & Connection

Providing peer support, meetups (coming soon), and warm spaces where nobody has to struggle or nod along in silence.

💡 Practical Education

Equipping employers, families, and venues with the practical PACE communication framework and awareness workshops (coming soon).
