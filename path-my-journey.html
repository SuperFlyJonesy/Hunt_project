route My Journey: Jason (Founder)

From 15 years of silent struggle in Bristol to overcoming stigma, discovering assistive listening tech, and founding the Hearing Loss Initiative.

Jason (Founder)

Founder, Bristol Hearing Loss Initiative & Community Advocate

"I lost my hearing around the age of 30. Looking back, I think it came down to a combination of things: spending too much time in nightclubs standing right next to heavy sound systems, paired with a mild undiagnosed hearing vulnerability from when I was young."

The Slow Creep: Hearing loss rarely happens like switching off a light. It fades gradually—first consonants like s, f, th drop away, then noisy rooms become a wall of incomprehensible sound.

For nearly fifteen years, I struggled in complete silence. In social settings and work meetings, I learned to master the art of the 'nod and smile'—pretending I caught every word while my brain was running at 200% capacity trying to piece syllables together.

sentiment_very_dissatisfied The Hidden Cost of Listening Fatigue:

Living in denial creates profound cognitive exhaustion. By the end of every working day, the constant lip-reading, context guessing, and acoustic strain left me utterly depleted and withdrawing from friends and family.

Stepping into the Audiology department at St Michael's Hospital in Bristol was terrifying at first. Admitting you need help feels daunting, but getting properly diagnosed and fitted with digital hearing aids gave me my life back.

Pioneering Field Trial

Bristol Temple Meads Station: Auracast Experience

Testing cutting-edge direct broadcast audio on station platforms.

check_circle Reconnecting With the World:

  • Speech Clarity Restored: Hearing individual voices in restaurants without straining.
  • Environmental Sounds: Birds singing, rain on the window, and footsteps.
  • Pioneering Tech: Participating in Bristol's first public Auracast broadcast trial at Temple Meads station!

Over 62,220 adults in Bristol live with significant hearing loss, yet so many suffer in isolation. I founded the Hearing Loss Initiative to build the bridge that was missing: connecting clinical NHS pathways with real-world accessibility, community coffee meetups, and venue ratings.
