Give your staff the confidence to communicate with people who have hearing loss – whether they’re colleagues, customers, patients, students or visitors.

Our Hearing Loss Initiative training is built and delivered by Jason and our specialist team, drawing on years of community advocacy and lived hard-of-hearing experience.

A practical, plain‑English session that shows your team how to:

  • Spot early signs that a customer or colleague might have hearing loss.
  • Use the PACE communication framework (Patience, Attention, Clarity, Eye Contact) effortlessly.
  • Run meetings, interviews, and events that don't exhaust or exclude hard-of-hearing colleagues.
  • Understand assistive technology (Roger On mics, TV streamers, live captioning, and Auracast).

Our sessions center around the PACE principles – simple, memorable habits that transform workplace communication:

  • P – Patience: You may be asked to repeat yourself, possibly numerous times. Please be patient. Rephrase if necessary.
  • A – Attention: Pay close attention to the environment and the individual. Ask if you need to adjust the surroundings to reduce background noise or improve lighting. Confirm they caught key details without putting them on the spot. Keep alternative methods in mind. Options like written notes, live smartphone captions, or basic signing exist as useful backup tools when verbal communication breaks down.
  • C – Clarity: Clear speech is essential! Avoid shouting, speaking clearly is key.
  • E – Eye Contact: Face the person so that they can read your lips and facial expressions. Most hard-of-hearing (HoH) people do this unconsciously.

Physical PACE pocket cards and desk handouts are provided to all workshop attendees.

  • 1 in 5 adults has hearing loss – including valuable employees and loyal customers.
  • Reduces costly workplace misunderstandings, frustration, and communication fatigue.
  • Fulfills Equality Act 2010 accessibility duties and demonstrates genuine inclusive culture.
  • Improves customer service ratings and accessibility reputation across Bristol.

Our team visits your workplace or venue in the Bristol area. We tailor examples to your specific setting (front desk, clinic, office).
