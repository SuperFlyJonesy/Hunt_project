Built and delivered by Jason and our specialist team, our sessions draw on authentic lived hard-of-hearing experience to deliver practical, plain-English training that shows your team how to:

  • Spot Early Signs: Recognize when a customer, patient, or colleague is struggling to hear without putting them on the spot.
  • Apply the PACE Framework: Use Patience, Attention, Clarity, and Eye Contact to make everyday interactions effortless.
  • Run Inclusive Meetings: Structure in-person and video meetings (Teams, Zoom) to eliminate listening fatigue and communication barriers.
  • Understand Assistive Tech: Learn how hearing loops, Roger microphones, live captions, and Auracast empower hard-of-hearing staff.

Our training centers around the PACE principles — simple, memorable habits that transform workplace communication:

  • P – Patience: You may be asked to repeat yourself, possibly numerous times. Please be patient and rephrase key words if needed.
  • A – Attention: Pay close attention to the acoustic environment. Reduce background noise, improve lighting, and use written notes or live captions as natural backup tools.
  • C – Clarity: Clear speech at a steady pace is essential. Avoid shouting, which distorts facial expressions and lip patterns.
  • E – Eye Contact: Always face the person so they can read lips and facial cues. Most hard-of-hearing people rely on visual speech reading unconsciously.

Physical PACE pocket cards and digital desk guides are provided to all workshop attendees.
