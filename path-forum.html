forum Private Community & Support Forum

A safe, verified, and troll-free space for hard-of-hearing adults, caregivers, and allies across Bristol to connect and share advice.

To protect our members from spam, aggressive advertising, and online harassment, we maintain our private discussion community through Patreon.

Community Core Values:

  • Zero Judgement: Share hearing challenges, awkward social moments, and emotional hurdles in complete confidence.
  • Peer-Led Guidance: Real insights on NHS models, audiologist experiences, and assistive gadgets from people who live it daily.
  • Privacy First: Your conversations and medical details are never sold, scraped, or indexed by search engines.

When registering for private forum invites or local meetups, we ask for your name and broad Bristol postcode. Here is exactly why:

📍 Localised Meetup Booking

Knowing where members live allows us to book accessible quiet coffee venues close to where our community is clustered (e.g. Bedminster, Easton, Clifton, Southmead).

📊 Securing NHS & Council Funding

Aggregated geographic data helps us prove demographic need to Bristol City Council and NHS health boards to secure grants for loop systems.

🎙️ Monthly Supporter Q&A

Direct access to guest audiologists, acoustics engineers, and community organizers.

🔍 Early Venue Audits & Assessments (Coming Soon)

Vote on which Bristol pubs, restaurants, and cinemas our team should audit for accessibility next.
