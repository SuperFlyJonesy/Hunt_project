Auracast™ broadcast audio is a revolutionary standard built on Bluetooth® LE Audio. Unlike traditional Bluetooth, Auracast allows a single transmitter to broadcast audio to an unlimited number of receivers simultaneously.

Bristol Temple Meads Trial

Bristol Temple Meads is serving as a pioneer public rail station trial of Auracast, providing real-time safety alerts and announcements directly to compatible hearing aids and cochlear processors.
