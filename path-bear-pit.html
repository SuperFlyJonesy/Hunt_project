Auracast & Next-Gen Accessibility

Auracast™ broadcast audio is a new standard built on Bluetooth® LE Audio. Unlike traditional Bluetooth, Auracast allows a single transmitter to broadcast audio to an unlimited number of receivers simultaneously.

Bristol Temple Meads is serving as a pioneer public rail station trial of Auracast, providing real-time safety alerts and announcements directly to hearing aids.
